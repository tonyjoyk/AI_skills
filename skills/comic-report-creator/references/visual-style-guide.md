# Visual Style Guide — Editorial Comic Report

## Intent
Create a calm, approachable, hand-drawn comic that feels like an illustrated field report or editorial webcomic.

## Style
- Hand-drawn editorial illustration.
- Natural, simplified human proportions.
- Fine-to-medium dark ink outlines with slight organic variation.
- Subtle watercolour and coloured-pencil texture.
- Warm off-white paper.
- Soft, muted colours.
- Expressive but restrained faces and gestures.
- Minimal contextual backgrounds.

Avoid photorealism, 3D, glossy vector art, heavy gradients, elaborate graphic-novel shading, exaggerated children's-cartoon proportions, and decorative texture that reduces legibility.

## Traditional comic grammar
- Rectangular panels and clear white gutters.
- Dark, clean panel outlines.
- Obvious left-to-right, top-to-bottom reading order.
- White speech balloons with dark outlines and clear tails.
- Small rectangular narration boxes for context/time transitions.
- No visible panel numbering by default.
- No infographic cards, dashboard tiles, poster headers, floating statistics, or decorative icon clusters.
- A diagram may appear inside a scene as an object a character is explaining.

## Composition
Give each panel one main purpose. Keep faces/hands visible when expression or gesture matters. Reserve uncluttered space for balloons. Do not place text over faces or busy patterns. Vary proportions only to improve rhythm. Do not crowd the page to preserve an arbitrary panel count. Avoid repeating a group portrait at the end if it adds no new information.

## Palette
Starting values, adjustable to the project:
- Warm off-white: `#F5F1E8`
- Muted coral: `#EBCFC8`
- Soft blue: `#D4DFE8`
- Soft lavender: `#DED6E8`
- Soft green: `#D5E1D8`
- Muted teal: `#D5E2DE`
- Soft yellow: `#E4D9B7`
- Dark ink: `#292724`
- Muted navy character labels: `#36516F`

Reduce saturation rather than increasing it. Maintain contrast. Use yellow narration boxes for meaningful context or passage of time, consistently.

## Labels and typography
- Speech balloons: white, dark outline, clear tail.
- Narration: pale yellow where context/time needs emphasis.
- Character labels: one consistent muted-navy treatment with white text.
- Meeting captions: simple white or warm-paper caption with dark outline.
- Use legible comic lettering or clear hand-lettered style.
- Keep line lengths short and spacing generous.
- Never shrink copy to fit an overcrowded balloon; edit copy or layout.

## Single-image production
Plan the entire page before generation. Minimise text without changing meaning, keep balloons spacious, use exact approved wording, inspect every word after generation, and correct malformed text. If the user asks for editable/separately typeset artwork, use a different workflow.
