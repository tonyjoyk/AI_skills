## Differentiation review
Compare silhouette, face shape, hairstyle, glasses/accessories, clothing colour/pattern, expression, and body language. Make distinctions clear without caricature.

## Cultural and regional accuracy
Do not infer ethnicity, religion, or clothing from a name alone. Do not use national stereotypes or assume traditional clothing in professional settings. Use photographs and user context as primary references. Ask when an important visual detail is unknown. Keep each sheet specific to its country/project.
